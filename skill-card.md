## Description:

Plans prioritized content topics and produces structured briefs from product knowledge, competitor signals, and the content calendar without drafting articles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand-side tech-product marketing teams use this skill to identify content gaps and prepare one to three prioritized topic briefs for human review before drafting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Planning reads workspace files that may contain internal customer information.

Mitigation: Limit access to relevant planning files and flag internal customer cases for redaction.

Risk: Duplicate or weakly supported topics may be recommended.

Mitigation: Check the last 12 published or planned items and verify source material before human approval.

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may invoke planning unintentionally.

Mitigation: Tighten trigger phrases before broad deployment.
